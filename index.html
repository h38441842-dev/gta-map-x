Answers & Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the State of Leonida, GTA 6 map size, coordinates, and community cartography.

What is the State of Leonida Interactive Map for GTA 6?
State of Leonida is a community-driven high-resolution interactive GIS mapping project for Grand Theft Auto VI (GTA 6). It features 21K deep-zoom cartography, 2,850+ triangulated landmarks from official Rockstar Games trailers and leaked development data, dual-reality OpenStreetMap comparisons, and in-game coordinate measurement tools.
How big is the GTA 6 map compared to GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2?
Based on rigorous community GIS triangulation (including 21K deep-zoom satellite telemetry), the State of Leonida is estimated at approximately 125 km² of surface landmass. This makes it roughly 2.55 times larger than GTA 5's Los Santos (~49 km²), 1.66 times larger than Red Dead Redemption 2's playable map (~75 km²), and over 13 times larger than the original 2002 Vice City map.
What major cities and counties are included in Leonida?
The map contains Vice City (Vice Beach, Downtown, Little Haiti, Starfish Island, Venetian Islands), the industrial metropolis of Port Gellhorn, the agricultural county of Kelly County with Ambrosia, Leonard County, Mount Kalaga National Park, Lake Leonida, and the expansive southern Leonida Keys connected by the Overseas Highway.
Who are the main playable characters and what are their story roles?
Grand Theft Auto VI stars dual protagonists Lucia Caminos and Jason in a modern criminal partnership reminiscent of Bonnie and Clyde. Leaked gameplay confirms character swapping during missions and open-world exploration, with shared weapon bags, trunk equipment storage, and individual narrative questlines spanning from correctional facilities to high-stakes urban robberies.
How are the in-game coordinates calculated before launch?
Community cartographers map in-game positions by utilizing the 3D Cartesian coordinates (X, Y, Z axes) revealed in the 2022 development footage. These points are combined with road sign mileage markings, camera focal length mathematics from Trailer 1, and horizon landmark alignment vectors to establish accurate geographic triangulation.
Can I calculate driving distance and travel times across the map?
Yes. The app features an interactive GPS Route & Travel Calculator accessible from the toolbar. You can select any two locations (e.g., Vice Beach to Port Gellhorn or Ambrosia to the Keys) and calculate real-time driving times for Supercars, Helicopters, Airboats, and on-foot exploration.
What vehicles, supercars, and aircraft are confirmed on the map?
Official trailers and leaked telemetry confirm classic and modern vehicles including the Grotti Cheetah, Pegassi Torero XO hypercar, Bravado Gauntlet Hellfire, Pfister Comet, swamp airboats, Marquis luxury yachts, police interceptors, and amphibious seaplanes operating throughout Leonida's waters.
When is GTA 6 releasing and on what platforms?
Rockstar Games has officially confirmed a Fall 2025/2026 release window for PlayStation 5 (including enhanced support for PS5 Pro) and Xbox Series X|S. A PC release is anticipated to follow following historical Rockstar release cadence.
Will the map update when Rockstar Games releases Trailer 2 and new screenshots?
Absolutely. The State of Leonida cartography database connects to our live master API. Any newly revealed locations, highway networks, or screenshot discoveries from upcoming official Rockstar Games releases are reviewed by community GIS editors and published to the live 21K interactive map in real-time.
